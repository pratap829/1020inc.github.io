VIHAAN'S PROPOSED STARTING POINT

You don't need to know the technical details.

Based on the business challenge, VIHAAN will consider the following design questions and mark them for verification. These are proposed patterns, not confirmed facts about your systems.

01
Detect a potentially abandoned cart

Use an available cart activity signal and an approved inactivity rule. The actual event and timing must be confirmed with the commerce team.

02
Check for a completed purchase before contact

Use an authoritative purchase signal where available. The freshness and reliability of that signal must be verified.

03
Respect identity, consent and contact rules

Confirm the customer can be identified and contacted through an eligible channel; suppress contact when required data or permission is missing.

04
Measure outcomes and guardrails

Track recovery outcomes alongside opt-outs, duplicate contacts and other agreed safeguards.
